How to play: click a red piece, then click a highlighted square. Captures are compulsory; ordinary pieces capture forwards or backwards. Kings can travel and capture across multiple empty squares. Multi-captures are completed in one turn. In friend mode, take turns using the same device: Red moves first, then Black. Choose an AI level when playing against the computer.
